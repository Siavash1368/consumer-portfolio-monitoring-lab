## 3 · Why
- **Deterioration, not mix.** Split by vintage, the change *inside* vintages contributed +210 bp; mix shifts (with interaction) contributed -61 bp. The largest single driver is the 2007 vintage (90+ rate 0.68% → 4.60%).
- **New lending is masking it.** The 2009 vintage grew to 18.5% of balance at a 90+ rate of 0.01%, which holds the headline rate down.
- **Seasoning.** At 22 months on book, cumulative ever-90+ by vintage: 2003 0.6%, 2004 1.3%, 2005 1.9%, 2006 3.0%, 2007 4.5%. The 2007 vintage is the worst at that age — 7.4x the 2003 vintage — so this is underwriting quality, not just seasoning.
- **Denominator.** On balance 12 months earlier the 90+ rate is 2.36% vs 2.12% on current balance; the book changed +11.3% over the year.
- **Macro.** US unemployment went from 5.6% to 9.5% over the same 12 months.
